# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1, as its Code of Conduct. Please read the full text here:

- https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this community a welcoming, respectful and
harassment-free experience for everyone, and to act in ways that keep the
community open, inclusive and healthy.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness a problem, please report it confidentially to the
maintainer through GitHub:

- Open a private security advisory:
  https://github.com/gastonsantosgnz/pdf-latex-ai-converter/security/advisories/new
- Or contact the maintainer: [@gastonsantosgnz](https://github.com/gastonsantosgnz)

All reports will be reviewed and handled promptly and fairly.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and upholding these standards and may
take appropriate, fair corrective action in response to behavior they consider
inappropriate or harmful, up to and including a temporary or permanent ban from
the project's spaces.
